## Change and unchanged requirements

An immutable semantic-generalization action mode defaults to `enabled`.
`AggregationReplay --semantic-generalization exact_only` returns every occupied
original terminal category with its truthful count/support to the same metric
and policy evaluator. It prevents semantic coarsening without changing the
hierarchy, the hard semantic-loss ceiling, category-support requirements or
spatial/temporal policy. Unsupported candidates fail as complete footprints;
rare types are never silently omitted. Positive selective-suppression allowances
are rejected in this mode. Configuration/evaluator mismatches are rejected in
selection, cell coordination and publication revalidation.

Fixed 20 m/0.4 s, regional static20 and the five adaptive execution variants each
receive an OFF counterpart. Three additional fixed OFF windows only support the
regional composition and are excluded from headline/MIA denominators. The same
72 recordings, split roles, target tracks, auxiliary knowledge and IN/OUT pairs
are reused. No new movements, MongoDB capture or policy relaxation is involved.

The new binary is built separately under `simulator/build-semantic-ablation`.
Original replay/Simulator binaries and historical studies remain unchanged.
Before native edits, historical code, results, models and public Git anchors were
authenticated into an explicit local content-addressed archive. The new bridge
distinguishes archived producer code from current native code. It additionally
binds every actual capture proof, configuration and observation file to the
frozen original source input and saved validation receipt before reconstruction.

## Maximum-delay diagnostic finding and authoritative correction

The first actual independent audit stops after 13 complete groups on a genuine
maximum-delay mismatch. The frozen collector inserts Counter keys even when a
scheduled public-cell timestamp contains zero observations. Its max(keys) can
therefore include an empty timestamp. The first affected case reports 3,100 ms,
while the true maximum among its 21 retained observations is 1,700 ms. Mean,
p50, p95 and p99 match exactly. This Python utility diagnostic is separate from native
publication-policy checks; no policy bound is changed.

The original V1 auditor, failure execution and diagnosis remain immutable. A new
private V2 auditor independently reconstructs both definitions. It requires exact
agreement with every stored legacy delay field, separately verifies positive-
observation profiles, and rejects a wrong stored maximum or wrong regional nested
profile. All 13 original fixture ASTs and comparison tolerances are unchanged;
15 focused fixtures pass independently before the full V2 audit.

Across all 2,448 rows, 28 maxima are inflated by 100–1,400 ms: 18 historical ON rows and
10 new OFF rows; TRAIN 14, CAL 6, TEST 8. None of the 432 auxiliary rows or regional
compositions is affected. Weighted means and the p50, p95 and p99 quantiles, together with independently
derived nested positive-observation profiles, all pass. The separate public correction
provides corrected mean recording maxima by method/world/split with unchanged
available Ns. Original summary.json retains its frozen scheduled-key maximum;
use retained-delay-maximum-correction.json for actual retained-observation maxima.
The main tables and figures use unchanged mean delay.

Full111 and Scalar31 predictors and public controls are constructed from public
output and auxiliary knowledge before the utility diagnostic. No delay-field

## Joint interpretation of privacy and utility

Across the seven pairs, Full111 AUC is 0.565–0.605 ON and 0.500–0.514 OFF; Scalar31
is 0.579–0.608 ON and 0.500–0.515 OFF. All fourteen predeclared paired OFF-minus-ON
95% intervals lie below zero. Immediate Semantic First has Full111 difference
−0.0693359375 with interval [−0.12990722656249998, −0.03271484375].

This is lower measured membership discrimination for the frozen attack, paired
with omission of more than 99.8% of input. It must not be described as a useful-
data-preserving privacy improvement. Six OFF models have constant balanced
TRAIN optima; a chance result for those classifiers is not a proof that their
recipient streams contain no information. The privacy scope is membership of
all observed contributions of one operational track in [0,300 s), with the same
quarter-count exact position/time auxiliary sample. It is not identity protection,
all visits by a person, an attribute-disclosure attack, or differential privacy.
No second/differencing attack is introduced in this task.

The original ON adaptive-versus-regional primary Full111 contrast remains
+0.0126953125, interval [−0.04396972656249998, +0.072265625]. Additional MIA benefit
or equivalence of adaptive ON over regional-static ON is not established by this
interval. The semantic-action ablation instead quantifies the coverage/risk cost
of requiring all original category labels exact under the same hard policy.

Root visually inspects all four actual final PNGs: utility, exact/estimated
category queries, MIA AUC and paired contrasts. Labels, zero markers, chance/zero
references and legends are legible; no clipping or overlapping text is found.
Conditional Ns and the positive-observation maximum-delay addendum remain required
when interpreting retained detail, error or delay. J includes omission plus
taxonomy distortion and is not an independent privacy metric.
